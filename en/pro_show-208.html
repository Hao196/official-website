Irradiation Under-beam Equipment

1. Fine-Wire (Small-Diameter Tube) Irradiation Equipment

Capable of irradiating heat-shrinkable tubes with a diameter of Ø0.3-8.0 mm (all dimensions refer to the bore before expansion, same below), and radiation crosslinked wires with a minimum cross-sectional area of 0.03 mm2 (US standard 32 AWG).

2. Large-Conductor (Tube) Irradiation System

Capable of irradiating heat-shrinkable tubes (or wires) with a diameter of Ø9-120 mm and a wall thickness of less than 5 mm, heat-shrinkable wrapping tapes of various specifications, heat-tracing cables of various specifications, and radiation crosslinked wires and cables with an outside dimension not exceeding 3×185 mm2.

3. Sheet Irradiation

Capable of irradiating continuous sheets, infusion films and thin films with a maximum width of 1300 mm and a thickness of 0.3-6 mm.

4. Rail-Mounted Platform Irradiation System

Capable of irradiating heat-shrinkable tubes with an inner diameter of Ø6-350 mm and a length of less than 1600 mm, cable termination accessories of various specifications such as finger caps, sealing caps and rain sheds, and electronic components including transistors, small thyristors, resettable fuses, semiconductor wafers and PTC chips.

It is also used for irradiation color treatment of gemstones, crystals and similar items.

5. Radiation Sterilization

Capable of irradiating medical supplies and devices, pharmaceutical materials, hygiene products, cosmetics, pet food, daily necessities, and food products for preservation.

Technical Specifications

Electron beam energy: 1.5 MeV - 5.0 MeV (adjustable from 1.5 MeV to 3.0 MeV)

Beam current: 0-50 mA

Scanning width: 1600 mm

Scanning frequency: 150 Hz (X), 3000 Hz (Y)

Scanning non-uniformity: <±10%

Energy instability: <±2%

Beam current instability: <±2%

Operating vacuum: greater than 7×10-5 Pa
