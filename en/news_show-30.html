Common Problems and Production Process of Cage Stranders

The cage stranding machine is a new type of machine that has developed rapidly in recent years. For imported models, the design typically selects a programmable controller as the key control device for the machinery and equipment. To better meet the requirements of high output, the fully automatic models generally adopt a double-head connecting-rod design. Combined with robots, pneumatic control devices and spare parts, they achieve automatic transverse movement, automatic winding, automatic cutting, automatic control, and automatic loading and unloading on both sides. Now, let us look at the common problems and the production process of the cage stranding machine.

The cage stranding machine offers high production efficiency and greatly reduces reliance on manual labor; it is especially suitable for production and processing sites with high output. As is well known, this machine model integrates many new technologies such as CNC machine tools, pneumatics and optical switches. In addition, the operating technicians can monitor several such machines at the same time, and the manufacturing quality is relatively stable. Because the cage stranding machine and its parts use many non-standard and customized components, the whole maintenance process is quite complex, and the repair cycle becomes longer when problems occur.

1. Coil specification problem

When winding a single turn with the cage stranding machine, the current of the magnetic powder clutch that controls the tension of the high-speed cage winding machine is set to 0.45 A. After the coil is wound, the specification of the B end is slightly larger, beyond the process requirements.

2. Cause analysis of the coil specification problem

It was found that if the current related to the tension resistance of the cage stranding machine is set to 0.45 A, the tension resistance during winding is too small, causing non-uniform force on the wire in the winding drum. The wire between the upper drum and the wire seat is not ideally horizontal but loose and tilted, so when the drum rotates, due to inertia, the drum speed is high and the wire slips more, resulting in uneven support of the wire in the winding, insufficient tightness of the coil, and the B end coil exceeding the technical requirements.

The cage stranding machine is a widely used model and specification, also known as the CNC automatic winding machine. It can wire automatically and can perform different winding resistance adjustments according to the mechanical system. Many manufacturers have made innovations in terms of function and use. After upgrades, the range of products in use continues to expand. As a common model on the market, it has advantages such as high efficiency, easy maintenance and good value for money. It has long been a very refined machine model, and its price is far lower than that of the countless automatic chain-movement machines actually used.

Production precautions for cage stranding machine wire:

1. The bare copper wire should be of good standard, neatly arranged, round and smooth, bright on the surface, and free of oxidation.

2. The electrical configuration and instrument displays of the machine should be normal, the rotating parts should run smoothly, and there should be no loose parts.

3. Adjust the drawing tension to a suitable value according to the specification of the stranded steel wire.

4. Select the middle copper wire to pass through the counting needle.

5. Thread the wires reasonably and separately from the wire clamp plate, and adjust the wire clamp plate and the stranding die to suitable positions.

6. Select a suitable pressing die to ensure that the copper wire is smooth with no jumping strands or scratches.

7. The rollers that the copper wire passes through should rotate flexibly, and there should be no loose copper wire at any roller.

8. There should be no foreign matter inside the machine, the take-up machine should be firmly installed, and the protective signal feedback (sensor detector) should be intact.

The above introduces the common problems and production process of the cage stranding machine. If you want to know more, please feel free to contact us at any time.
