Featured Products

Advanced core technologies engineered to meet the production demands of high-speed, high-frequency, new-energy and other cutting-edge cables

Vertical Cage Strander

Utilizes a vertical rotary structure for the cabling process, uniformly twisting multiple insulated conductors into a cable core at a preset lay length.

Provides stable, uniform stranding in a compact, space-saving design with smooth operation. Adaptable to cabling requirements for different core counts and cross-sections, and suitable for the production of power cables, control cables and telecommunication cables.

Cable Stranding Equipment for Electric Vehicles (Charging Piles)

Built for the new-energy vehicle industry, this equipment specializes in stranding charging-pile cables and large-cross-section flexible conductors, meeting the process requirements of new-energy cables for flexibility, bending resistance and high-current transmission.

Features precise tension control and uniform stranding and layering; suitable for stranding EV / charging-pile cables and silicone and specialty flexible cable conductors.

USB3.1, Type-C Extrusion Line

Used for extruding the insulated cores and outer jackets of high-frequency data cables. Supports ultra-fine coaxial structures and thin-wall insulation to meet the precision requirements of high-frequency signal transmission.

Delivers high extrusion precision with stable inner and outer diameters, accommodating FEP, PE, PVC and other materials; widely used in the production of USB3.1, Type-C and high-frequency ultra-fine cables.

Horizontal Single/Double/Triple-Layer Taping Machine

Used in the cable taping process to apply single, double or multiple layers of taping materials such as aluminum foil, mylar and non-woven fabric, in either overlap or butt-wrap configuration.

Offers adjustable taping tension with neat, smooth layering, and enables combined longitudinal and transverse wrapping of double-sided laminated aluminum foil; suitable for taping the shielding layers of high-frequency cables and the isolation layers in cabling.

QF-50 Single-Core, Twin-Core and Drop Fiber Optic Cable Production Line

For the production of FTTH drop cables and indoor optical cables, covering processes such as fiber pay-off, strength-member laying and sheath extrusion.

Delivers stable tension control and is compatible with sheath materials such as PVC and low-smoke halogen-free flame-retardant compounds; applicable to butterfly drop cables, self-supporting butterfly cables and indoor distribution cables.

Chemical Foaming Extrusion Line

Produces low-density foamed insulation layers through chemical foaming, used for the extrusion forming of coaxial cable cores.

Provides uniform, dense foaming with stable dielectric performance that significantly reduces transmission loss; suitable for insulation production for coaxial cables and various RF and communication cables.
