Handling Common Problems of Precision Stranding Machines



1. Composition and main uses of the precision stranding machine

This precision stranding machine can be used for the bunched stranding of small cross-section wires. It is composed of a single-twist machine, a separating wire plate, a twisting cage and an electric motor.

2. Preparations before production with the precision stranding machine

1. Place the special tools and measuring instruments on the workbench, and check that the measuring instruments are zeroed.

2. Check that the precision stranding machine is normal, especially the stop/start device and the protective-cover power-cut safety switch. If any abnormality is found, report it to the maintenance personnel for repair immediately.

3. Prepare the semi-finished products according to the production plan, check that they have passed inspection and meet the process standards, mount the qualified semi-finished products onto the single-twist machine, fit the taper sleeve, and adjust the tension to the correct setting.

4. According to the quantity, lay diameter and lay direction specified in the process standard, mount the change gears, adjust the lay direction, select the combining die according to the process standard and install it. Prepare the take-up reel as required, check that it is intact, and mount the take-up reel on the take-up frame.

5. Pull a leader wire through the guide roller and the guide tube all the way to the traction wheel inside the cage, wrap it around the traction wheel 3 to 4 turns, and fix one end of the leader wire in the take-up reel.

3. Usage essentials of the precision stranding machine

1. Pass each single wire separately through the auto-stop device and the separating wire plate, pull them to the front of the combining die, twist the wire bundle into one strand, pass it through the combining die, pull out 0.5 to 1.0 m, and fix the combining die on the die seat.

2. Join the wire strand to the leader wire, close the protective cover, start the machine slowly, and when the joint reaches the take-up reel, wind two turns onto the take-up reel. Stop the machine, cut off the unqualified part of the joint, pull out the leader wire, and fix the qualified joint on the take-up reel.

3. Start bunched stranding for 20 to 30 m; check according to the requirements whether the wire diameter of the individual wires has been drawn down, whether the lay direction conforms to the process standard, whether the core wires are glossy and free of looseness, and only when confirmed correct may production continue.

4. While the precision stranding machine is running, observe frequently the running condition of the reel of the single-twist machine and the winding condition of the take-up reel. If any abnormality occurs, stop the machine immediately, find out the situation and handle it, and then restart.

5. After production is completed, fill in the product manufacturing card carefully, place the products in the pending-inspection area, and after the process inspector confirms they are qualified and stamps the card, place them in the qualified-product area pending final inspection.

4. Quality standards

All the bunched strands must not be welded as a whole bundle; individual wires may be welded, but no more than two joints are allowed in the same cross-section, the distance between two joints should be not less than 300 mm, and the joints must be firm and smooth. The bunched core wires must not be missing wires, broken, loose or overlapped; the individual wire tolerance, lay diameter and lay direction in the core should conform to the process standard.
