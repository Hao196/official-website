Do You Know About Installing a Taping Machine on a Shaftless Lifting Payoff?

Since no stranding is required, the process by which transmission line pairs are formed into cables is in fact completed on the wrapping machine. The tension control of every part of the wrapping machine is the key factor that determines its quality. For SFP high-speed transmission lines, the wrapping machine should have at least the following components: an active pay-off unit with stable tension, a concentric double-layer winding unit, a hot-melt unit, and a take-up unit with stable tension. The technical control points of each functional unit are analyzed below.

The strap length is adjusted by the strap length adjuster on the front plate. If the strap is too short, it is easy to wind up around the operator's hands. In this case, firmly grasp the strap end and pull the strap out along its direction on the wrapping machine; however, the strap length must be re-adjusted to ensure the next tying operation proceeds normally. If the strap is too long, it is hard to position, and the loose strap is easily tangled, resulting in inaccurate tying or strap breakage. Maintenance:

1. Keep the inside of the machine clean and dry at all times.

2. Prevent moisture or dirt from accumulating on the machine tabletop.

3. The power cord should be kept clear of moving parts and should be coiled up when not in use.

4. Keep the machine running normally and select quality raw material strapping straps.

Main features of the wrapping machine equipment.

1. The wrapping machine adopts separate motor control, achieving high speed, low noise and no oil leakage, which also keeps the workshop environment clean. Alternatively, it can use ground-shaft drive (the gearbox is a high-speed six-speed gearbox offering forward, reverse and neutral gears, linked to the ground shaft of the main drive, providing high speed, low noise and high production capacity).

2. The wrapping device has a flat structure. The strap payout angle is adjusted by guide rollers, each reel can be adjusted independently, and the strap tension is controlled by mechanical friction with automatic adjustment.

3. Braking is by pneumatic disc brake, giving a good and smooth braking effect.

4. The caterpillar-type haul-off unit has a built-in 4-speed gearbox and is linked to the winding machine through encoder signals, ensuring a stable and reliable winding pitch.

5. The wrapping head is fitted with a high-strength integral base plate, making installation and adjustment easy and operation reliable. It eliminates the defect of foundation settlement and ensures the machine's accuracy during transport.

6. The wrapping machine has a scientific and rational structural design, well-selected materials and fine workmanship, with a low failure rate, and it can meet the requirements of long-term, high-intensity continuous production.

The wrapping machine equipment is mainly used for the non-metallic wrapping of various cables. After parts adjustment, it can also be used to produce copper tape shielding, thin copper tape and steel tape armoring.
