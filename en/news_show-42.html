Safety Guidelines for Extruder Machine Operators



The extrusion machine equipment production operator is the person responsible for maintaining and servicing the machine and increasing the working service life of the extrusion machine. Every operator must understand that reducing the frequency of inspection and repair of the extrusion machine, reducing the frequency of part replacement, and increasing the working service life of the extrusion machine are the principal measures for lowering the cost of plastic products and improving economic and social benefits. The equipment being able to work in its best condition for a long time, and the stability of plastic quality and total output, are the principal criteria for ensuring that manufacturing enterprises enjoy higher profits.

Operators who work carefully in accordance with the safety operating procedures and are dedicated and responsible give the equipment the best maintenance and care. Therefore, before new workers come on duty, they must first learn and memorize the safety operating procedures of the extrusion machine; only after passing the operating process assessment can they truly come on duty and operate independently.

To work independently and become a qualified extrusion production operator, the following matters require special attention:

(1) At shift handover, remind the next shift of the problems arising from this shift's production equipment and hand over the details clearly; keep proper handover records of the shift's total output, the product quality and the safety tools used in production.

(2) At shift takeover, after completing the handover procedures, first check the manufacturing quality of the product, inspect the quality of the raw materials used for production, and check the quantity of materials in the extrusion machine hopper. If any problem is found, report it to the person in charge immediately.

(3) Check whether the barrel and process control temperature of the extrusion machine is within the process temperature range stipulated in the production standard. It may be adjusted appropriately according to the product quality status and your own practical operating experience.

(4) Walk a full inspection lap around the production equipment, check the working condition of each transmission part while the equipment is running, see whether the rotation is stable and normal, listen for any abnormal noise at work, and check whether the heating position ambient temperature works normally.

(5) Look at the gauges to check the main motor working voltage and the ammeter needle on the operating control cabinet; at this time the ammeter needle should be within the normal rated voltage value range.

(6) Production proceeds normally. While the screw of the extrusion machine rotates steadily, the plastic products are extruded at a uniform, stable speed from the machine; from time to time, check the surface quality of the equipment, or take some products to check whether the various indicators of the equipment are qualified.

(7) In the event of a sudden power failure during work, first turn off the power switches of the main motor, electric heating and feeding system, and turn the knobs of the speed regulating switches to the zero position. When the power supply is restored, first heat and raise the temperature of the barrel; after the process temperature is reached, hold the temperature for a period of time. Manually turn the motor drive belt to test the frictional resistance of the screw rotation; if it is relatively easy to turn, run the motor at low speed and start working. If the ammeter needle exceeds the rated voltage value or the screw speed ratio is unstable, stop immediately, look for the cause (it may be that the material temperature in the barrel is low), investigate the root cause of the fault according to the rules and eliminate it before restarting.

(8) If the machine is shut down for a long time or production is stopped, and the material inside the barrel is a polyolefin-based raw material, it does not need to be removed. If the barrel contains acrylic resin or other materials, the material in the barrel must be removed; then disassemble the screw and remove the adhered material from the screw and the barrel; after the finished products in the screw and barrel are thoroughly cleaned, apply a layer of rust preventive, wrap the screw well, and hang it vertically in a dry, well-ventilated place.

The above is the safety note for equipment production operators described by the extrusion machine manufacturer; we hope this article can help you.
