Stranding Machine Manufacturer Explains How to Adjust the Spindle of an Automatic High-Speed Stranding Machine

High-speed stranding machines have evolved from hand-cranked, to automatic, to fully automatic operation over the past half century. A fully automatic high-speed stranding machine must necessarily incorporate a wide range of automation technologies, mainly including control systems, transmission control, motion control, industrial touch screens and sensors. Driven by market demand, fully automatic high-speed stranding machines are now the most widely used type, although the market has long since taken on a flourishing and highly diversified landscape. So what are the adjustment methods for the spindle of a fully automatic high-speed stranding machine? Our stranding machine manufacturer will introduce them below.

The stranding machine manufacturer

Many purchasers of fully automatic high-speed stranding machines simply use the equipment according to the layout and configuration set at delivery, just as they would follow a car dealership's arrangement. Some customers, however, need to change the position or function of certain components to suit their own production process. When a fully automatic high-speed stranding machine is designed and manufactured, a certain amount of space is generally reserved to facilitate convenient maintenance and the dismantling of components. Customers can use this space to make certain improvements, but every improvement must stay within the maximum limits permitted by the equipment. Making blind, arbitrary modifications to the equipment can damage it.

The spindle of a fully automatic high-speed stranding machine is the drive shaft that applies rotational force to turn the wire being stranded. Machines of different sizes have different spindle configurations. Spindle adjustment generally covers the following aspects: the shaft outlet position, coaxiality adjustment, and supplementary winding process settings. The shaft outlet position usually needs adjustment when, during the winding stage, the clearance between the spindle and the machine table does not meet the production process requirements, since every production stage requires a certain working space. When adjusting the shaft outlet position, pay attention to the size of the opening on the main frame and whether the adjustment will affect the normal operation of other components. Coaxiality adjustment is common: after a period of operation, the coaxiality between the winding reel and the mold pin on a fully automatic high-speed stranding machine may deviate, and it must be inspected and adjusted as soon as possible.

Adjustment of the wire traversing device mainly covers the traverse interval and the rotation diameter. The traverse interval is adjusted when the starting winding point does not match the winding process. The usual method is to move the machine's homing sensor, but bear in mind that the movement must not exceed the machine's mechanical starting point; leave as much clearance as possible so that the equipment cannot cause a mechanical collision when returning to its home position. Since adjustment methods differ from one stranding machine manufacturer to another, customers should contact their supplier in good time before making adjustments and master the key adjustment steps.

There are many kinds of magnet wire coils, using a wide variety of winding wires and with diverse requirements. For some magnet coil products with special requirements, domestic manufacturers were once forced to give up one after another. After years of absorbing and digesting the technology, the process capability of fully automatic high-speed stranding machines has been steadily improving, and even models with special requirements can now be handled much more easily. As stranding machine manufacturers' production capacity grows, customization lead times are also getting shorter.

The above is what the stranding machine manufacturer has explained about spindle adjustment methods for fully automatic high-speed stranding machines. We believe that through today's content you now understand these adjustment methods, and we hope it is useful to you.
