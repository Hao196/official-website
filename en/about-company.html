Company Profile


Dongguan Qingfeng Electrical Machinery Co., Ltd. was founded in 2000.

We are a high-tech enterprise that, on a global scale and over the long term, has been dedicated to the R&D, manufacturing and service of wire, cable and optical fiber cable equipment. We can provide ideal solutions according to customers' special requirements, and offer corresponding professional technical solutions based on the cable structure and actual process requirements.

As one of China's top cable equipment manufacturers, we operate both domestic and international sales teams, along with R&D, production and manufacturing, quality inspection centers and an independent after-sales service department, providing a full range of pre-sales, in-sales and after-sales services. There are currently four companies, including Qingfeng Electrical Technology (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd., Dongguan Shatian Shengfeng Electrical Machinery Factory and Kunshan Fengsheng Electrical Machinery Co., Ltd. With an in-house factory area of 9,000 square meters, fixed assets of over RMB 15 million, more than 90 employees, 12 technical staff, and large CNC lathes and milling-boring machining centers.

We have over 20 years of manufacturing experience, more than 15 years of in-house factory history, and more than 10 years of export experience. So far, our products have been sold to over 30 countries around the world. We will, as always, provide the highest quality services to all customers, and work hand in hand with them to grow together and create a better tomorrow.
