Maintenance and Care of Cage Stranders

After the cage stranding machine is installed and the foundation has set, begin installing the power wiring. After all installation is complete, lubricate the rotating parts and start the motor. After running at low speed for one hour, check whether the fasteners are loose, and tighten them if so. After all checks are finished, check high-speed operation and no-load operation. Then let us learn about the maintenance and care of the cage stranding machine. The cage stranding machine can twist 19 soft copper wires; by removing one or two transmission components, it can also produce 7 soft copper wires, or it can be changed over to bunching 12 wires.

Install the wire reel, pull the wire to the take-up reel, close the protective mesh, start the motor, adjust the speed gradually, and check the tension of the elastic friction band mounted on the friction wheel of the drum. If the friction band tension is too high, the wire may break; if the friction band tension is too low, the wire on the spool is liable to spring loose after stopping. Therefore, before use and during use, check the rotation of the spool and its condition after stopping.

When the coil is full, stop the main machine, turn the transmission connection to the raised position, activate the lifting and lowering functions, remove the winding shaft, mount an empty reel, and raise it to the transmission connection position. During the whole process of loading and unloading the full board, be sure to note whether the loading and unloading positions of the transmission connection parts are aligned.

Each time the cage stranding machine is started, or when the wire drum is changed, the speed controller must be reset to zero, so as to prevent the speed controller from being damaged during straight-line motion, or the main machine stopping or accidents occurring during operation.

Maintenance and care of the cage stranding machine.

The cage stranding machine has a frame structure, and its main body is a rotating body. The main body may vibrate slightly because the weight of the wire drums is not well balanced. When the cage stranding machine is running, pay attention to the lubrication of the transmission parts in each section, and add lubricating oil once every 10 days of production, so as to avoid wear of the rotating parts due to lack of lubricating oil.

Long-term use with the wire drum between the pulling friction wheel and the bevel gear can cause damage to the rotating bearing; replace damaged bearings at any time.

Long-term use of the friction transmission will wear out the friction pads and discs; replace them promptly. The friction discs should be re-machined to restore the surface profile, so that the friction coefficient meets the friction and sliding requirements, ensuring that the disc works normally and drives the take-up shaft to rotate.

1. Keep the inside and outside of the cage clean; each folding surface, the lead screws, gears and frame, etc. should be free of oil stains and scratches; each part should have no oil, water, air or gas leakage; remove chips and debris.

2. To avoid affecting the wiring of the rebar cage rolling machine, it should be placed in a dry room rather than a humid environment. If it needs to be placed outdoors, it must be put on flat ground. In addition, cover the rebar bending machine with cloth to prevent it from getting damp.

3. Lubricating oil must be added regularly to the parts that require it.

4. Turn off the power supply, and switch off the power switch before leaving the rolling cage machine.

5. Place the machine's lever in the idle position; if it is not set to the idle position, it will be harmful to the engine. Place all control levers in the neutral position.

6. The operator of the cage machine must have skilled technique, be familiar with the performance of the drum cage, and be able to solve abnormal situations in time to avoid damage to the rebar bending machine.

7. The cage stranding machine should be inspected before operation; check whether each station and each part is within the normal working range, and if abnormal, report for handling in time.
