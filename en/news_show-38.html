Advantages and Applications of Cage Stranders



The cage stranding machine is a very good application product, and it also has many unique advantages and properties. Now let us look at the advantages and the scope of application of the cage stranding machine.

It has good mechanical properties and electrical insulation performance, and its stability gives good water resistance and oil resistance. In addition, it is also easy to process and form. In order to further promote the development needs of cable technology, the formulation and properties must be continuously improved, so as to effectively enhance heat resistance and voltage level and better improve the low-temperature resistance and aging resistance of the material.

Therefore, the polymer products produced from synthetic materials have good plasticity, which better extends the service life of the cable. As a result, the product has more advantages and properties during application, and has gained wider recognition and praise from users of the cage stranding machine.

Compared with traditional rebar processing equipment, the cage-type rolling machine not only breaks the tedious point-by-point operation mode of the traditional bending machine, but also effectively realizes the automation of rebar bending and significantly improves the positioning accuracy of the rebar bending angle. Therefore, in modern infrastructure construction, the popularity of the rebar cage rolling machine is being promoted and the utilization rate of the equipment improved.

Scope of application of the cage stranding machine:

1. Bridge pile foundation sites: the CNC rebar cage rolling machine can bend 6-40 mm or 6-50 mm rebar into the various shapes required for bridge construction.

2. Rebar straightening and adjustment: the CNC rebar cage rolling machine straightens and adjusts plain carbon steel, deformed rebar and other geometric shapes required by the workers.

3. The CNC rebar cage rolling machine can perform vertical bending of rebar after casting or tying, such as for shear wall topping, building capping columns, bending the main bars at the head of a rebar cage, and aligning main bars after pile splitting.

The specific advantages of the cage stranding machine:

1. Simple operation. Workers can do the job after simple training.

2. Low labor intensity. Large-diameter rebar cages have obvious production advantages; 2 to 3 people can complete the whole operation of the machine.

3. Strong adaptability. The welding spacing of rebar cages with main bar diameters of 12 mm to 50 mm can be adjusted automatically.

4. Fast welding speed. The automatic welding hand can weld a weld point in 1 to 2 seconds, and the processing efficiency is 3 to 4 times that of the traditional manual mode.

5. Stable quality. The weld points are reliable, the distribution of main bars is highly accurate and consistent, and the spacing of the stirrups is uniform, providing the basis for fast butt-joining of the rebar cages afterward.

6. The rebar cage rolling machine has a high degree of automation. It is computer controlled and touch-screen operated; the speed and the travel speed can both be infinitely adjusted, the joints can be welded automatically, and automatic welding is realized.
