Chairman's Message


 Chairman's Message

Dongguan Qingfeng Electrical Machinery Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise that, on a global scale and over the long term, has been dedicated to the R&D, manufacturing and service of wire, cable and optical fiber cable equipment. The company has always adhered to the business philosophy of "integrity builds the brand, quality wins the world." After more than ten years of development, it has grown from a small enterprise into one of China's top cable equipment manufacturers. Following the principles of customer orientation, warm professionalism, active innovation and mutual benefit, we have always stayed focused on the R&D and manufacturing of high-quality cable equipment, providing customers with first-class equipment tailored to their individual needs and the most ideal cable solutions.

We adhere to the development philosophy that "technology is the root, innovation is the soul, and talent is the foundation." While upholding standardized corporate management and market-oriented operations, we also practice people-oriented, humanized management. Every employee who joins our team undergoes strict technical assessment and systematic technical training. We value our employees' learning ability and continuously guide and develop them through practical work. As technology accumulates and the company grows, our team keeps expanding and management becomes increasingly scientific. We shape the corporate spirit, keep customers and the team close to heart, shoulder our social responsibilities, and work together to create a better life for society.

We have always operated on the principle of "quality first, customer foremost," following the guideline of "seeking progress through technological innovation and development through scientific management." We keep forging ahead and improving. We warmly welcome and deeply appreciate the guidance of friends in the industry, and will, as always, work sincerely with our customers to grow together and create brilliance.
Chairman of Qingfeng Electrical Machinery: Mr. Zheng Qingjun
