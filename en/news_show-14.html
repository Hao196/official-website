New Energy Vehicles Drive Cable Market Growth with Huge Potential

As electric vehicles gain in popularity, the EV charging pile industry is also booming, and charging performance has become a matter of widespread concern. Fast charging demands high power and high current, yet a cable that carries current for long periods tends to heat up. The quality of charging pile cables is therefore critical, because it is directly related to charging safety.

The voltage rating for high-voltage cables was originally 60 V. In response to market changes and demand, this has now risen to as high as 1,500 V, and the increase from 60 V to 1,500 V places far higher demands on cables. Likewise, automotive conductors have grown from 25 mm² to high-voltage cables of 50 mm², 75 mm², 95 mm² and 120 mm². As cable cross-sections have increased, the required voltage withstand ratings have risen accordingly.

Traditional wire and cable machinery manufacturers have never produced conductors of such large cross-sections and cannot achieve the corresponding voltage withstand ratings. Conversely, the companies capable of manufacturing large-cross-section conductors are not part of the automotive supply chain and know little about the product requirements and delivery standards of the automotive industry. Both the companies within the automotive supply chain and the cable manufacturers outside it are searching for ways to break through.

Manufacturing large-cross-section high-voltage cables to such demanding standards requires advanced equipment. With more than 22 years of experience in the production of wire and cable machinery and over 60 national invention and utility-model patents, Qingfeng is one of the leading domestic manufacturers of professional high-end cable equipment. We can custom-build complete extrusion lines to customer requirements, suitable for new-energy charging pile cables, new-energy special cables and related applications.

As new energy vehicles become widespread, the supply gap in high-voltage cables will draw many conventional cable producers into the automotive supply chain, while the demands on companies already in that supply chain will keep growing. With strong national emphasis on the new energy vehicle market, the share of new-energy high-voltage cables in China's cable market is expected to rise. At the same time, the automotive high-voltage cable market will go through a phase of survival of the fittest and accelerated consolidation of resources.
