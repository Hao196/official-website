Number of Mica Tape Layers and Overlap Rate for Taping Machines



For mica tape wrapping on the wrapping machine, it is difficult to give definitive figures for how many layers should be wrapped and what the overlap rate should be. Generally, mica tape manufacturers recommend wrapping 2 layers, with the overlap rate equal to or slightly greater than 50%.

Because cable products differ in material properties and model/specification, only the product designer can give definitive values for how many layers to wrap or how much overlap is needed to pass the fire-resistance test, based on the results of repeated tests. Of course, the more wrapping layers, the higher the product pass rate, and vice versa. However, this not only wastes mica tape - it also increases the insulation and sheath outer diameter of the cable, which in turn raises the plastic quota, increases manufacturing cost, and reduces the competitiveness of the product. The number of wrapping layers should therefore be determined by the user's requirements, the cable structure, the complexity of the process, the voltage class and the test results.

Meanwhile, with a given number of wrapping layers, the overlap rate is also a very important indicator, because it determines the actual number of wrapping layers. For example, if one layer is wrapped with an overlap rate of 50%, the actual number of wrapping layers is 2. If the overlap rate is below 50% - say 25% - the actual wrapping layer is 1.5, meaning some places have 2 layers and others have 1 layer. Obviously, the 1-layer positions are liable to breakdown. If two layers of mica tape are wrapped with overlap rates both below 50%, the single-layer positions of the inner and outer wrap layers may overlap each other, so that those positions actually have only two layers of mica tape and are therefore more liable to breakdown than positions with four layers.

In process terms, it is very difficult to achieve an overlap rate of exactly 50%. In actual use, bending and other factors can also cause the overlap rate to fall below 50%. Therefore, in actual production, the overlap rate should be controlled at slightly greater than 50%.

The above is what the wrapping machine has explained about the number of wrapping layers and the overlap rate; we hope it is helpful to you.
