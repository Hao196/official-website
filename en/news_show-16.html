New Energy Vehicle Wiring Harness

In 2020, China produced 25.225 million vehicles. At an estimated wiring harness value of RMB 2,000 per vehicle, the annual output value of China's automotive electronic wiring harness products exceeds RMB 50 billion. The automotive wiring harness industry is highly dependent on the automotive sector. Most brand-name automakers have established mature and stable component supply systems; international automakers — particularly the German, American and Japanese manufacturers — subject their component suppliers to strict assessment and evaluation. Their long-standing high standards for components have kept the relationship between wiring harness suppliers and automakers relatively stable.

In recent years, as international automakers pay increasing attention to cost control, localised sourcing of automotive components has steadily strengthened, and a number of excellent domestic wiring harness manufacturers have emerged. Through many years of accumulated product technology and synchronous-development experience, these quality local companies have significantly strengthened their overall capabilities and, backed by timely and effective service and reliable product quality, have gradually entered the supplier systems of international automakers.

In the fast-growing new energy vehicle sector, the wiring harness is one of the most important components, mainly comprising body harnesses, BMS (Battery Management System) harnesses and energy storage harnesses. Because the lithium batteries used in new energy vehicles are rather unstable, the battery management system, as the core technology, plays a decisive role. Of all these harnesses, the BMS harness is the most critical one, functioning as the nerve centre of the vehicle.

BMS harnesses are subject to demanding requirements: they must be short, compact, light, thin and flexible, while also carrying high currents and providing dust-proof, water-proof and interference-resistant performance.

In electric vehicles, the connectors and cables of the high-voltage harness are critical interconnection components during vehicle operation. The main hazards affecting high-voltage harnesses are overheating or burning, in addition to harsh environmental exposure, shielding performance, and the risk of water or dust ingress.

China's new energy vehicle industry has made remarkable progress, and new energy vehicles of every kind have gradually entered our daily lives. These include battery electric vehicles, extended-range electric vehicles, hybrid electric vehicles, fuel-cell electric vehicles, hydrogen-engine vehicles and other new energy vehicle types. In any new energy vehicle, whether a hybrid or a fully electric model, the high-voltage system is one of the core parts, and its importance is self-evident. As the medium that interconnects the components of the high-voltage system, the high-voltage wiring harness likewise plays an indispensable role.
