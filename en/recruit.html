Our View on Talent:

Being competent in your own job is what makes you a talent,Those who keep learning, innovating and forging ahead are outstanding talents

Talent is the primary resource for a company's survival and growth, Qingfeng is open to talent of every kind,and gathers the very best.Regardless of academic background,as long as you work with your own diligent hands,to give full play to your intelligence and talent,and keep breaking new ground in our business,are all equally essential to our ongoing development,as indispensable outstanding talents.

Our Talent Strategy

We firmly believe: first-class talent is the foundation of a first-class enterprise.

Qingfeng adheres to“technology as the root,innovation as the soul,and talent as the foundation” as its business philosophy, always treating talent as the foundation for entrepreneurship and growth,for competition,and for development. The company has fostered respect and dedication,fair competition,and a fine working climate that values knowledge and talent. With its distinctive talent recruitment strategy,broad career prospects,an excellent environment for talent development,effective incentive mechanisms anda people-centeredcorporate culture, Qingfeng has become a platform where talented people converge. First, we create a sound platform and room for development for talent------retaining talent through career opportunities; Second, we retain talent through competitive compensation; Third, we foster a positive cultural atmosphere,constantly reinforcing internal affinity,and cohesion-----retaining talent through culture.These “three ways to retain talent” constitute the core of our human resource management. In the selection,training, and appointment of talent,we uphold fairness,impartiality,and transparency,and have established a scientific talent management system,so that every capable,and ambitious individual can bring their abilities into full play in building Qingfeng's business,and realize their aspirations.

   

We hope more professionals will join our team, more people to follow Qingfeng's development.
