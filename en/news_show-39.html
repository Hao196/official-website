Taping Machine Manufacturer Explains the Coiling & Packaging Process Procedure

The wrapping machine manufacturer explains the operating procedures of the wrapping machine coiling and packaging process.

The packaging machine is divided into single-layer, two-layer and three-layer types. Wrapping tapes (mica tape, cotton paper tape, aluminum foil, polyester film, etc.) are wound onto the core wire through the rotation of the reel/turntable. It is mainly used for the wrapping of insulated cores for power cables, control cables, optical cables and so on.

Its features are low energy consumption, stable and reliable operation, and simple operation.

Process preparation:

1. Place the tools and measuring instruments on the workbench and check that the measuring instruments are zeroed.

2. Check that the equipment is normal; if there is any abnormality, report it to the maintenance personnel for repair immediately.

3. Prepare the semi-finished products according to the production plan, check that the semi-finished products are qualified and meet the process standards, and mount the qualified semi-finished products onto the pay-off stand.

4. Select the wrapping die core according to the process standard, install the wrapping die plate, and reset the length counter to zero.

Operating procedures

1. Pass the wire end through the guide roller and pull the length counter to the wrapping die core, then fix the wire end.

2. Start the machine; after the wires are arranged neatly, start the linked run while keeping the wires arranged neatly.

3. After the wire is wrapped to the required length, tie it firmly with a three-strand fiber rope, and then lower the wrapping plate and the wrapping core.

4. Wrap the packaging wire of the new coil with packaging film, and put the corresponding identification in place according to the specification and length.

Quality standards

1. The wire surface is smooth and glossy, with no scorched material, eccentric core or mechanical damage.

2. The products after coiling must be arranged neatly, without disorder.

3. The model, specification and length on the identification must be consistent with the products; mistakes are prohibited!

The wrapping machine manufacturer explained the operating procedures of the wrapping machine coiling and packaging process; we hope it is helpful to you.
