Stranding Machine Manufacturer Explains How to Adjust the Spindle of an Automatic High-Speed Stranding Machine



High-speed stranding machines have gone through half a century of evolution from hand-cranked, to automatic, to fully automatic. A fully automatic high-speed stranding machine must necessarily apply many kinds of automation technologies, mainly including control systems, transmission control, motion control, industrial touch screens and sensors. Driven by market demand, fully automatic high-speed stranding machines are now widely used, but the market has long since presented a flourishing scene of many varieties. So what are the adjustment methods for the spindle of a fully automatic high-speed stranding machine? The stranding machine manufacturer will introduce them below.

The stranding machine manufacturer

Many purchasers of fully automatic high-speed stranding machines simply use the equipment according to the setup and configuration established at the time of purchase, just as they would follow a car dealership's arrangement. Some customers, however, need to change the position or function of certain components of the fully automatic high-speed stranding machine to suit their own production process. When designing and manufacturing, a certain amount of space is generally reserved for convenient maintenance and the dismantling of components. Customers can use this space to make certain improvements, but all improvements must remain within the maximum values permitted by the equipment; making arbitrary, blind modifications to the equipment will damage it.

The spindle of a fully automatic high-speed stranding machine is the drive shaft that provides rotational force to turn the wire being stranded. Machines of different sizes have different spindle configurations. Spindle adjustment generally includes the following aspects: the shaft outlet position, coaxiality adjustment, and additional winding process features. The shaft outlet position usually needs adjustment when, during the winding stage, the clearance between the spindle and the machine table does not meet the production process requirements, since every production process requires a certain working space. When adjusting the shaft outlet position, pay attention to the size of the opening in the main frame and whether the adjustment will affect the basic operation of other components. Coaxiality adjustment is common: after the fully automatic high-speed stranding machine has been running for a period, the coaxiality between the winding reel and the mold pin may deviate, and it must be inspected and adjusted as soon as possible.

Adjustment of the wire traversing device is generally the adjustment of the traverse interval and the rotation diameter. The traverse interval adjustment is made when the starting winding point of the machine does not match the winding process. The method is generally to move the machine's homing sensor. Note that the position movement must not exceed the mechanical starting point of the equipment; leave as much clearance as possible, so as to ensure that the equipment cannot cause a mechanical collision when returning to its home position. Because different stranding machine manufacturers produce machines with different adjustment methods, customers should contact the stranding machine manufacturer in good time before making any adjustment, so as to master the key adjustment steps.

There are many kinds of magnet wire coils, using various winding wires and with diverse requirements. For some magnet coil products with special requirements, domestic manufacturers once gave up one after another. After these years of technological digestion and absorption, the process capability of fully automatic high-speed stranding machines has been developing steadily, and even the models with special requirements can now be handled much more easily. With the increase in the production capacity of stranding machine manufacturers, the customization cycle is also shortening.

The above is the explanation by the stranding machine manufacturer of the spindle adjustment methods for fully automatic high-speed stranding machines.
