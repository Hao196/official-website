Safety Operating Procedures for Cable Stranding Machines and Common Faults



1. Safety operating procedures of the cable stranding machine:

1. Before starting work, put on protective gear, and wear no more than two gloves on each hand.

2. Be familiar with the machine performance and operating techniques. You may participate in operating the stranding machine after arrangement or approval by the team leader; apprentices must operate under the guidance of skilled workers.

3. Before starting the machine, check whether the mechanical and electrical quantities are normal. If there is any fault, eliminate it in time; otherwise do not start the machine. Pay attention to the lubrication and cleaning of the equipment, and do not add oil while the machine is running to avoid danger. The equipment should be wiped clean once a day and washed once a month.

4. When the drum is lifted with a crane, check before lifting that the sling is properly secured to the drum and that the hook is firmly fastened, and control the lifting of the crane. One person should be responsible throughout each lifting operation, and pay attention to tying the chains and hooks on the machine to avoid accidents.

5. When the coil is placed into the stranding machine, immediately lock the locking device; otherwise, the coil may become loose and cause equipment and personal accidents. Before starting the machine, close the protective guard, and make sure there are no obstacles within the rotating range of the machine before starting.

6. The protective guard may be opened only when the machine has come to a complete stop. Working on the machine is prohibited. When the machine is rotating, stepping on the machine is prohibited.

7. Operators should care for and look after each other. Before starting the machine, they must sound the alarm and confirm it is safe before starting.

8. If any abnormal sound is heard from the running machine, stop immediately for inspection, and restart only after the obstacle is removed.

9. Operators should check the butt welding machine; the welder must have a grounding wire, and eye protection should be used during operation.

10. Disconnect the power supply after stopping.

2. Faults that can occur with the cable stranding machine

During the whole application process of the cable stranding machine, due to unscientific operation or other natural factors, the twisting direction of the cable stranding machine may become wrong, the twisting lay diameter may be unqualified, or even disordered take-up winding and wire compression may occur.

Wrong twisting direction and unqualified twisting lay diameter (mainly slightly too large) are mainly caused by judgment errors, including errors in judging the twisting direction. During the operation, the operators should strengthen their sense of responsibility, strictly execute the process card, improve roving inspection, and avoid rejects and waste caused by human factors.

If the wiring is disordered or wires are compressed, it is mostly caused by the following situations: stranded products are generally supplied on trays. Large cross-sections should be arranged one by one, and especially the bottom wire must be arranged neatly. When the cable reverses its traversing direction, because of the one-time stop, special attention should be paid to ensuring there is no very large gap between the upper and lower layers, and the arrangement spacing should be suitable. If it is too large, wire compression is easily caused.

For the above situations, we can choose the following methods to handle them:

First, adjust the take-up tension of the cable stranding machine. When the cables are arranged manually, the most suitable method is to arrange them flexibly.

Second, according to the core diameter specification, adjust the lay diameter of the cable. Arranging the first layer is the key, and it must be arranged neatly. For the following layers, manpower should be arranged when adjusting each layer to ensure the arrangement quality.

Third, improve the sense of responsibility during operation, observe the cable laying quality at any time, and adjust immediately when problems are found. Pay attention to fixing the two wire ends and the edge spacing.
